Simple Process

How to order

From reference image to finished jewellery all via WhatsApp. No complicated forms needed.

Start on WhatsApp
01
Send your reference
Share a photo or image of the jewellery design you want from Instagram, Pinterest, or anywhere. WhatsApp us directly.
02
Get a quote
Our artisan will assess your design and provide a price estimate based on weight, metal type (gold/silver), and complexity.
03
Confirm & we begin
Once you approve the quote, we start crafting. We'll share progress updates via WhatsApp throughout the making.
04
Pickup or delivery
Collect from our Liluah store or arrange for safe delivery. Your bespoke piece exactly as imagined.

Our Story

A legacy of fine craft

Maa Ambey Jewellers has been serving families in Howrah for generations. Every ornament that leaves our workshop carries the blessing of Maa Ambey and the dedication of skilled craftsmen who treat your gold like a sacred trust.

We specialize in custom-made jewellery you bring the vision, we bring the craft. From bridal sets to everyday elegance, in gold and silver, plain and stone-set.
